From One Class to Five Locations

It started with a single class and a belief that martial arts could change children's lives. Fifteen years later, Train Taekwondo Schools is one of East Yorkshire's most trusted children's activity providers.

We've watched shy children become confident leaders. We've seen families train together for years. We've helped children on the autism spectrum thrive through our internationally-acclaimed Spectrum Skillz programme.

And this September, we're growing again — with new Ninja Skillz classes launching across the region.

2011
Train Taekwondo Schools founded by Gavin Cook, 5th Dan Black Belt
2015
Expanded to multiple venues across East Yorkshire
2019
Launched Spectrum Skillz — specialist programme for children on the autism spectrum
2024
Introduced Ninja Skillz — specialist classes for children aged 3–7
2026
Expanding to 5 locations across East Yorkshire and York
