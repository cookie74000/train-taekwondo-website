Junior & Family Taekwondo

Start a journey you can be proud of.

Junior Taekwondo gives children a clear, rewarding route from their first white belt onwards. Family Taekwondo makes it even more special: adults and children train together, learn the same syllabus and work towards gradings side by side.

  • Junior students can train independently from age 6
  • Children can train from age 5 when alongside a parent
  • Real TAGB Taekwondo techniques, patterns and gradings
  • Supportive coaching for complete beginners

Questions answered

Everything you need to know

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first taster is completely free, with no pressure to join afterwards.

What should we wear?

Comfortable clothes are perfect for a first session. There is no need to buy a uniform before you try it.

Can parents train too?

Absolutely. Family Taekwondo lets parents and children train together in the same class and work towards gradings side by side.

What if we are unsure of the right class?

Book a taster at the nearest venue. Gavin will chat through the options and make sure you are in the best-fit programme.
